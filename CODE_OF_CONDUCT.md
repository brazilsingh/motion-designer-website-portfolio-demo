# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to code or to design.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, or sexual or discriminatory language or images.
- Don't share anyone's private information without their permission.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, report it privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile: https://github.com/brazilsingh. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who don't follow these rules.
